                    </p>

                            <h3 class="timeline-card-title">The Global Citizens’ Assembly Movement</h3>
                            <p class="timeline-card-summary">
                                Drawing foundational principles from the world’s first planetary citizens’ assembly at COP26 and the ongoing development of permanent global deliberation infrastructure, CTAI builds upon proven sortition and civic lottery methodologies.
                            </p>
                            <div class="timeline-key-outcomes">
                                <div class="outcomes-title">Key Milestones & Insights</div>
                                <ul>
                                    <li>Established the standard for transnational sortition, ensuring equitable representation across the Global South and North.</li>
                                    <li>Pioneered multilingual deliberation, local community assembly networks, and tech-enabled collective intelligence platforms (Assemblis).</li>
                                    <li>Demonstrated that humankind can reach nuanced consensus on complex existential challenges when provided structured learning and respectful dialogue.</li>
                                </ul>

                            <h3 class="timeline-card-title">Two Global Editions of PAIRS & the PAVE Case Book</h3>
                            <p class="timeline-card-summary">
                                The Participatory AI Research & Practice Symposium (PAIRS) united over 200 researchers, practitioners, and activists across the world to build the theoretical and empirical underpinnings of participatory AI governance.
                            </p>
                            <div class="timeline-key-outcomes">
                                <div class="outcomes-title">Key Milestones & Insights</div>
                                <ul>
                                    <li><strong>PAIRS 2025 (Edition 1):</strong> Established the global community of practice examining how public engagement shapes AI development and resistance.</li>
                                    <li><strong>PAIRS 2026 (Edition 2, New Delhi):</strong> Convened alongside the India AI Impact Summit, showcasing grassroots AI audits and frontline governance from Africa, Asia, and Latin America.</li>
                                    <li>Created the <strong>PAVE Case Book</strong> (pave.pairs.site), documenting real-world participatory AI methods and impacts from every continent.</li>
