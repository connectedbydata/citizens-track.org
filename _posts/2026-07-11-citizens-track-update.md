## **Share the Citizens’ Track…**

On Tuesday we launched our White Paper, a Citizens’ Track on AI: Alignment, Agency and Accountability.

We need your help to share the message that **participatory practice can address the current democratic deficits in AI governance**. 

Please do share our [posts about the paper on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/timgdavies_a-citizens-track-on-ai-governance-activity-7479888764643819521-nH8R?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAACbFGoBxo6HJ6_csEkVhqc727PXJvrWg1E) and [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/timdavies.org.uk/post/3mpya3m4pcs2v), or write your own reflections on what the idea of a Citizens’ Track means to you. 

Or, if you can think of someone who should know about the citizens track initiative, please drop them an e-mail or a WhatsApp with a link to the paper.

## **Partnership Hub for Participatory AI Governance**

![Informal meeting at the PAVE Expo Booth](/assets/posts/2026-07-11-IMG_6525.jpg)

We’re delighted to be a founding member of the Partnership Hub for Participatory AI Governance, launched within [the framework of the UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance](https://www.un.org/global-dialogue-ai-governance/en/ai-dialogue-partnerships-hub). 

On Tuesday we held a first informal meeting with representatives from Missions Publiques, Iswe Foundation, MetaGov, i4Policy and Socratus (participatory practitioners rooted across five continents) at the PAVE Expo Booth to explore how our different initiatives can collaborate.

We identified a key opportunity in coordination around activities for the 2027 Geneva AI Summit, bringing greater coherence to advocacy and messaging about the importance of public participation in AI governance. 

As the Partnership Hub becomes established, we’ll share more details of how you can get involved through this newsletter. 

## **Support the citizens’ track**

The citizens’ track is a partnership initiative, with many partners contributing in-kind time and support. 

Building on the response to our work in Geneva last week, we are working on plans to make sure we can take forward an ambitious agenda for public voice: both to resource the ‘institutional’ layer of the citizens’ track, and to support distributed community assemblies with different communities and in different parts of the world. 

We welcome any signposting to potential support, and [outreach about individual donations](mailto:tim@connectedbydata.org?subject=Tell%20me%20about%20how%20I%20could%20sponsor%20the%20citizens%20track).
