## **Whitepaper Launch: A Citizens’ Track on AI: Alignment, agency and accountability** 

We are delighted to be sharing [the citizens’ track whitepaper](https://citizens-track.org/2026/report/), setting out why and how participatory practice can address the democratic deficit in AI governance, and bring greater alignment with the public interest, agency for communities and accountability of action. The paper sets out the action needed to move from listening to public voices, to connecting with people through community assemblies across the world, and to ultimately embedding inclusive public participation in the evolving AI governance architecture.

[The full paper and the one page executive summary are available to read now](https://www.citizens-track.org/).

There's still time to [register to join us remotely for the launch of the paper](https://citizens-track.org/2026/06/25/join-us-in-geneva.html) in a fringe event of the UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance at 09:00 CEST on Tuesday morning. 

### Launching the PAVE Case Book

The whitepaper builds on a strong foundation: evidence from over 80 projects across the world that have explored participatory approaches to AI development, governance and resistance. Thanks to amazing efforts from everyone who has been submitting case studies and a fantastic research team, we are [really pleased to be launching the casebook website today](https://pave.pairs.site).

## **People in the lead**

I want to give the final word in this short citizens’ track update to the 11 members of the People’s Panel on AI, whose recommendations after spending a week learning alongside the 2023 AI Safety Summit, set us at Connected by Data on a path to explore how global participation on AI could be realised in ways that are both inclusive and impactful.

They called for global governance of AI “...to bring together citizens, impartial experts and government from across the world, and ensure regulatory collaboration that includes the global south.”
