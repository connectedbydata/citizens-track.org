### When

  * 3:00 PM – 4:00 PM Geneva (CEST)

### What to expect
During this call, we will:
* **Reflect on the UN Global Dialogue:** Share feedback and reflections on the recent UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance.
* **Look Ahead:** Discuss the draft workplan for the Citizens’ Track in the months ahead.
* **Updates:** Share updates on the PAVE Case Book, partner activities, and next steps for the governance and partnerships of the initiative.

We want to hear your thoughts, answer your questions, and coordinate together on how we shape the future of participatory AI governance.
