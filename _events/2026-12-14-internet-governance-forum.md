The the Citizens’ Track on AI will have a stand in the exhibition space at Internet Governance Forum in Nairobi, Kenya from 14th - 18th December - and Johnny, Susan and Octavia from the team will be there to share updates on our community assembly, evidence and policy work. 

We’re also looking for volunteers to help on the PAVE/Citizens’ Track exhibition stand during the week, and would love to connect CTAI community members who will also be in Nairobi that week. 

Get in touch with contact@citizens-track.org if you will be there!
