**Who gets to shape the conversation about artificial intelligence?**

How can we hold space for open community dialogues to explore different perspectives on AI?

What **questions**, **inputs** and **actions** could and should community assemblies on AI explore?

As part of the global [Citizens Track on AI](https://citizens-track.org/) project, we’re co-designing a toolkit for informed, active conversations that let communities explore and share views on the future of, or a future alongside, AI. 

Join us for a two hour workshop to:

* Learn about the Citizens’ Track on AI: putting people in the lead

* Suggest priority questions for Community Assemblies to address

* Explore the key knowledge that people need to have meaningful discussions on AI \- and and perspectives its important to be aware of

* Discuss approaches for community dialogue and action

## Who is this for?

Join us if you are interested in:

* Critical AI literacy

* Participation and public engagement

* Arts, cultural and community action responses to AI 

* Public voice in local and global AI policy making

This will be an interactive workshop. Participants should agree to abide by our code of conduct.

## Who are we?

The Citizens Track on AI is an initiative to embed public voice in the governance of data and AI at all levels. 

We created the [PAVE Case Book](https://pave.pairs.site) to map existing examples of public engagement on AI across the world.

Now we’re working to create a resource that can support many more grassroots communities to get together, deliberate, and share ideas and actions for shaping AI in the public interest.
