Citizens' Track partners, Coalition for the UN We Need, will present at the Global Digital Compact Localization Townhall – 'Localizing the Global Digital Compact: From Global Commitments to Local Action' on 18 November.

Mathilde Boddaert will talk about plans for a Geneva Hub to support grassroots public deliberation on AI, and build bridges into global dialogue.
